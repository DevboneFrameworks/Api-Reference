Reads the stored records of a person's key subjects, decrypted for that person: protected fields of the key subjects in plaintext, placeholders where their key is destroyed, and protected fields of anybody else withheld. Implemented by Devbone.Frameworks.Privacy.Requests.EventSourcing.

ExportAsync(IReadOnlyCollection<SubjectKeyReference>, CancellationToken)

Streams every record of the given key subjects once, in a stable order. The subjects come together, because a record of one, such as the person's, may carry fields of another, such as a contact the person owns, and both are disclosed to the person.
