Unprotects every tagged string of one or more JSON documents in place, resolving the keys the tags name through a registry in batches. This is the reader's half of field protection for anyone who holds a payload without its definition: the projection fold, a rehydration mapper, an export.

Remarks

The unprotector neither caches nor decides what an unreachable registry means; it lets SubjectKeyRegistryUnavailableException and SubjectKeyRegistryException pass through, so the caller keeps that decision. A destroyed or unknown key is not a failure: the tag's policy is applied. A value that does not authenticate throws FieldProtectionException, because a reader cannot repair an altered ciphertext by retrying.
